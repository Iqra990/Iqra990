## About Me

I am an MPhil graduate in **Food Safety & Quality Management** from Bahauddin Zakariya University, Multan, working at the intersection of **computer vision, deep learning, and food science**. My research applies AI vision systems to real-world food quality, authentication, and traceability problems — from **spoilage detection** to **grain grading** to **fraud-resistant supply chains**.

I build and evaluate object detection models (YOLO-family, RF-DETR) end-to-end: dataset collection, annotation, training, cloud vs. local deployment benchmarking, and validation against ground-truth food science metrics (e.g., Brix values).

- 🔬 Researching **smart food** and **smart agriculture** systems — AI-driven inspection, grading, and authentication across the food supply chain
- 🍅 MPhil thesis: AI vision-based classification of fresh vs. spoiled produce
- 🌾 Independent research: rice grain quality assessment and adulteration detection
- 🌸 Ongoing: blockchain + computer vision traceability to combat saffron ("Red Gold") fraud
- 🎓 Seeking postgraduate research opportunities in AI for food security and precision/smart agriculture

## Featured Projects

### 🍅 AI Vision-Based Detection of Fresh vs. Spoiled Tomatoes (YOLOv11)
MPhil thesis project. Built and annotated a custom tomato image dataset (Label Studio, YOLO format, bounding-box annotation), trained a YOLOv11 detection model, and validated results against fruit Brix (sweetness/freshness) values. Benchmarked cloud vs. local-machine training and inference, finding local deployment more efficient for real-time on-site food quality screening — achieving mean average precision in the high-80% range.

**Stack:** `Python` `Ultralytics YOLOv11` `Label Studio` `Google Colab` `Anaconda`
🔗 [Colab Notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1x04AfPZX0U8eog7YKmdXFs264CiB8MpV)

### 🌾 AI Vision-Based Rice Quality Assessment (RF-DETR)
Independent research project. Self-collected and manually annotated a 324-image rice grain dataset across two quality classes (damaged rice / high quality rice), then trained and evaluated an RF-DETR object detection model on Roboflow to automate rice quality grading and support adulteration screening.

**Results:** mAP@50 **88.8%** · Precision **91.1%** · Recall **84.1%**
**Stack:** `RF-DETR` `Roboflow` `Python` `Computer Vision`
🔗 [Roboflow Universe Project](https://universe.roboflow.com/iqras-workspace-tfdb2/rice-quality-assessment-2)

### 🌸 Combating Saffron ("Red Gold") Fraud — Blockchain + Computer Vision Traceability
*In progress.* Investigating an integrated blockchain and computer-vision framework to authenticate saffron and trace it through the supply chain, targeting adulteration and mislabeling fraud in high-value food commodities.

**Stack:** `Computer Vision` `Blockchain` `Traceability Systems`
